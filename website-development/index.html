Why a website

What your website
should make easier.

A website earns its place when it answers three questions quickly. Each one shapes a decision I make while building.

  1. Understanding what you offer

    Clear navigation and a front page that says what you do, who it is for and why it matters — in the first screen, not the third scroll. The page structure is planned around the questions your customers actually ask.

  2. Reading on a phone

    Most visitors arrive on a phone, so layouts, text sizes and menus are built for small screens first. Your hours, services and prices sit where people expect to find them — no pinching, no sideways scrolling.

  3. Getting in touch

    Every page keeps a visible way to reach you: an enquiry form, an email link or a WhatsApp button in a consistent place. The next step is never more than one tap away.

01 / BUSINESS WEBSITES

A home for your business

For local businesses and service providers — restaurants, clinics, shops, consultants — that are hard to find or hard to understand online.

02 / LANDING PAGES

One page, one job

For a single offer, campaign or product that deserves a focused page of its own.

One page that explains the offer clearly and leads to a single action — a call, a message or a booking — without the cost of a full website.

03 / PERSONAL PORTFOLIOS

Your work, in one place

For independent professionals and creators whose work is scattered across profiles and links.

Your projects, experience and a way to reach you, presented on a site you control instead of a profile you don’t.

04 / WEBSITE REDESIGNS

A fresh start, keeping what works

For businesses whose current site is outdated, hard to read on phones, or no longer matches the business.

We keep what already works — content, domain, anything performing well — and restructure and refresh the rest around your current offer.

Scope

What the work
can include.

The final list depends on the scope we agree on — this is the usual shape of a build.

  • Page structure and navigation
  • Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Visual styling aligned with your business
  • Placement and formatting of your content
  • Enquiry forms and contact links
  • Basic technical SEO setup
  • Image preparation and performance considerations
  • Browser and device checks
  • Launch preparation and handover

Basic technical SEO is the setup a site needs at launch — page titles and descriptions, sensible headings, a sitemap and pages search engines can index. It is not an ongoing SEO campaign, and I don’t promise rankings.

Anything outside this list — payment systems, booking platforms, custom dashboards — isn’t part of a standard build, but we can discuss it when scoping the project.
