Privacy & analytics

Updated 2 October 2026. ScaleUpBiz is run by Yatharth Rupesh Mehta. For privacy questions, email yatharth@scaleupbiz.co.in.

Project enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, the details you enter (your name, email, message and any optional business or website details) are sent through Formspree so I can respond and discuss your project. Do not include passwords, payment information or other sensitive details. Formspree handles submissions under its privacy policy.

Optional website analytics

If you choose “Accept analytics”, Google Analytics 4 measures page visits, scrolling, successful form submissions and clicks on email or WhatsApp links. Google may process browser and device information, referral information and approximate location, and uses cookies to distinguish visits. Our tracking code does not send names, email addresses, message contents or other form fields to Google. Query strings and URL fragments are excluded from the page URLs we send.

Analytics starts only after acceptance. Advertising storage and personalisation are disabled in our tag configuration. Google handles analytics data under its privacy policy. Reports help us understand which pages attract visitors and enquiries.

Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored in this browser. You can reject analytics and still use the website and contact form. Changing your choice to reject stops our analytics events and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies; the page reloads if analytics was already running. Previously collected data is not erased by changing this choice.

Hosting and external links

The website is hosted by Vercel. Published portfolio content is loaded from Supabase, and some fonts and media use external providers. These providers receive the requests needed to serve the website. Email, WhatsApp and Instagram links open their respective services, which have their own privacy practices.

Contact and requests

Contact me at the email above if you want to ask about, correct or delete an enquiry you have sent. I use enquiry details to respond and manage the project conversation.
